how it works

your screen, turned into
structured memory

01 —
capture
vizlog runs in the background and samples your screen. you pick which apps it can see.
02 —
structure
every minute gets turned into searchable text. what was on screen, what you were doing. stored as plain files on your disk.
03 —
retrieve
the MCP server plugs your context into whatever agent you already use. no new interface to learn.

memory efficiency

text, not video.
orders of magnitude smaller.

vizlog doesn't keep recordings. after each minute is processed, the video gets deleted. all that stays is text. from our own usage as founders, here's what that looks like:
per minute
~32 KB
a typical minute on your disk.
per day (10–12h)
~23 MB
a full working day. all searchable.
per year (5 days/wk)
~5–7 GB
a whole year. smaller than one hour of 4K video.
right now
<5 MB
the last 60 seconds of your screen, held in memory. always fresh.
